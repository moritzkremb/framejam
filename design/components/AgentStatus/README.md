# AgentStatus

One line next to the Send button that says, before you press it, whether Send will reach your agent right away.

- **listening** (blue, pulsing ring): your agent is waiting in `wait_for_feedback`, so Send delivers instantly.
- **working** (spinner): your agent has your comments and is making the next version.
- **not listening** (grey): your agent's turn has ended. Send still saves your comments, and the HandoffCard then tells you what to paste into the chat.
- **connected** (green): used on the home screen for setup health.

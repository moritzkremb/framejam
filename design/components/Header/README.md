# Header

The 56px bar on top of every screen. It never wraps, and it never holds more than three controls.

- **Home** (Reviews / Styles): the wordmark, a two-option Segmented control to switch, and the style in use on the right, shown as its colour dots and name. Tapping the style chip opens that style.
- **Inside a review or style**: a round back button and a two-line title (name, then a quiet subline like *Version 2 of 2 · 0:10 · 9:16*). On the right, the version menu (`v2 ▾`) and ⋯. Below 420px the version moves into ⋯.

The title truncates first. Everything else (source toggle, copy prompt, shortcuts) belongs in ⋯ or next to the thing it affects.

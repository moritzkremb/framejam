# StyleCard

A style in the gallery: a big preview, a one-click **Use** button, and its name.

- **Use** (white pill, top-right of the preview, always visible): makes this the style for your next video. It turns into lime **In use ✓**, the card gets a lime ring, and the header's style chip updates. Clicking *In use* again clears it; using another style switches.
- Clicking anywhere else on the card opens the style's page with all the details.
- The preview plays muted on hover. Format and pace sit bottom-right; below are the name, the style's colour dots and a two-line tagline.

# FeedbackDock

The box pinned to the bottom of a review. Writing feedback works like a chat input, and the Send button sits right under it.

1. **Anchor chip**: what the comment attaches to. *At 0:02* by default, *0:04 · on the yellow word* after clicking the video, *0:02 – 0:04* after dragging the filmstrip. × or *Whole video* detaches it.
2. **Text box**: Enter adds the comment to the list (it stays editable until you send); Shift+Enter makes a new line; `C` focuses it at the playhead.
3. **Send row**: the AgentStatus line says, before you press Send, whether it reaches your agent now ("Your agent is listening") or after you tell it ("Agent not listening · you'll tell it after sending"). **Send N to agent** sends every comment you've added since the last send. With nothing unsent, only the status line shows.

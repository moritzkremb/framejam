# The Ganimate example (craft only)

Public reference: [@_ganimate_ on X](https://x.com/_ganimate_/status/2115810508125446482), caption "Launches can
also look like Netflix Trailers!" (~53 s, 16:9). Credit the post when you mention it. Do not copy the footage, the
voiceover, the type, the music, the product shots, or anyone's likeness from it. Those assets are not in this repo
and must not be downloaded into a user's project.

What to take from the structure:

- Opens on mythology / lineage, in a vintage CRT, not on the product.
- Act 1 piles industry tension and scale; the picture stays archival and boxed.
- Music climbs, then a hard **1.5 s of black** (about 0:21.5–0:23) with no audio.
- The drop is a flip ("the unused thing"), not a logo. Type runs 1–3 words, white, on the beat of the VO.
- After the drop the frame is clean 16:9: epic landscape, then real product hero in the world (aerials, not a UI).
- Ends slow: mark + one CTA line + a boom.

Use that spine on the user's subject. Their B-roll, their VO, their hero, their logo. The playbook's own defaults
are in `beats.md`; this page is only so you can see where the spine came from.

# Trailer beats

The default is a 16:9 trailer of about 52 s. The voiceover writes the clock; the silence stays.

## The arc

| Time | Section | On screen | Words |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:05 | Hook | Archival or CRT; one image that feels like a lineage or a myth | A grand opening line, 4–10 words. Type: 1–2 words at a time |
| 0:05–0:17 | Problem | Fast scale: infra, cities, nature, industry tension | What's stuck, what's wasted, what's inevitable. Rising |
| 0:17–0:21.5 | Climax build | Tighten on the insight; last archival frames | One sentence that almost names the answer |
| 0:21.5–0:23 | Silence | **Black. Empty audio.** | Nothing |
| 0:23–0:34 | Reveal | Beat drop. Cleaner picture; driving cuts; kinetic type | The flip: the unused thing, the hidden-in-plain-sight move |
| 0:34–0:49 | Product | Full 16:9 hero of the real product or render | Scale, promise, what it is. Still 1–3 words per card |
| 0:49–0:53 | Outro | Slow; logo; one CTA | Name + "Build with us" / URL / waitlist |

A 45 s cut shortens Problem and Product, not the silence. A 60 s cut adds one more hero thought, not a second drop.

## Voiceover

- Write the VO first. One narrator. Authoritative, visionary, present tense. No "Hi, we're…" and no feature list.
- 70–110 words for ~52 s. Leave air at the silence and under the logo.
- Shape: myth or lineage → the tension → the insight → (silence) → the flip → the thing itself → the ask.
- Record it (user, or Kokoro locally, or ElevenLabs if they already pay). Don't start building on a placeholder voice
  if a real take is coming; a scratch read is fine for the first render if you say so.

**Quotes.** Optional, act 1 only, 2–3 clips of 1–4 seconds. Only files the user supplied, with rights. Super the
name if they want. Never generate a famous voice, never pull an interview off the internet, never draw a likeness
you weren't given.

## Type

- Bold clean sans (the style's display face, or the brand's, or Geist / Inter). White on the picture; one accent
  word at most.
- 1–3 words per card, centre, 96–160 px at 1080p. Swap on the spoken word or on a beat, not both fighting.
- Sentence case or lowercase; no exclamation marks. A period is fine for weight.
- The same line can sit in the environment (type over a sky, a lot, a road) if the shot is calm enough to read.
- After the drop, type can run a little faster. On the logo, one line only.

## Picture

**Act 1 (hook → silence).** Degraded on purpose: CRT scanlines, a little VHS tear on cuts, grain, chromatic fringe,
4:3 or 16:9 boxed inside the 16:9 frame. Colour can be sickly or desaturated. The box going away is part of the drop.

**Act 2 (reveal → out).** Clean, full-bleed 16:9. Match-cut or whip from the last archival frame into the new world.
Hero shots of the real product in a real place beat a turntable on black. Aerials, tracked inserts, beauty close-ups.
If all you have is a still render, push in slowly; don't fake a UI around it.

**Cuts.** 0.4–1.2 s in the problem and the reveal; 2–4 s on a hero that has motion; 1.5–2.5 s on the logo. Hard cuts
on the grid. A glitch cut only to jump decades, not on every shot.

## 9:16

Only when asked. Same edit, centre crop, type already centred so it survives. Don't add a second story. If a boxed
CRT shot dies in crop, replace that one shot; don't rebuild the trailer.

# How the reference reel is built

Reference: a 61 s, 9:16 Instagram reel by @aiwithremy (AI with Remy, https://www.instagram.com/reels/DeeroIoRpnr/),
posted with the caption "500 hours of short form, in one claude skill". One person talks to camera in a dim living
room and shares a free agent skill as an episode of a weekly series. Whenever he explains an idea, a white board
drops over the top of the frame and the idea is drawn on it as a clean diagram: a list, a tree, a mind map, a fan of
cards, a title. Use this breakdown to learn the pattern; never copy its footage, its lines, its series name or its
exact colours. The playbook's own look is in `references/graphics.md`.

Measured from the file: 1080×1920, 25 fps, 61 s, about 250 words (≈4.1 words a second, no pauses left in),
-14.2 LUFS integrated.

## Rules taken from it

- **Two layouts carry the reel.** Full-frame speaker for opinions and asides; the white board on top with the
  speaker slid down under it for every explanation. The board appears and disappears with hard cuts, ~45% of the time.
- **Diagrams, not decorations.** Each board shows the structure of what's said: steps → a numbered list, "it copies
  them" → a tree to identical outputs, "how to think" → a mind map, "three principles" → a fan where each card is
  revealed as it's named, results → an analytics panel. Things build up in sync with the words (one item, node or card
  per spoken phrase), roughly every second.
- **Plain UI on white.** White rounded cards with soft shadows on an off-white board with a faint vignette, near-black
  bold sans text, small grey mono labels, one electric blue for pills, lines and focus, red only for strikes and the
  badge icon, a lime marker on the number that matters, a handwritten script for notes and the series tag.
- **Real pictures for real things.** Thumbnails, profiles, the folder of reels and the analytics are screenshots of
  real pages; abstract ideas get diagrams. Older episodes appear as short cutaways.
- **Speaker stretches can run longer than in a pure motion-graphics reel** (up to ~4–5 s), held by punch-ins, an
  angle change or a physical beat (glasses on, a prop held up). Graphics return within a few seconds.
- **A series frame.** A badge names the weekly series once, early, over the speaker; the thing being shared gets its
  own title card.
- **Captions never stop.** 2–3 lowercase words at a time, white, a rounded mono face with a dark soft shadow and no
  box, centred on the chest; they keep running under the board.
- **The CTA is a keyword in the caption spot**: "comment" and the word in big quotes, held to the end.
- **Sound.** A light, steady music bed (~97 BPM by onset analysis) runs under the whole reel, roughly 20 dB under the
  speech peaks, with no gaps in the voice. Sound effects on the graphics weren't confirmed by listening; the playbook
  adds quiet pops, pen scribbles and whooshes so each graphic lands.

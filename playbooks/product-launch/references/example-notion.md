# Example: the Notion launch demo

An unofficial practice piece Moritz made to show the format: a Twitter-style SaaS launch video for a well-known
product, built only from real assets on its website. 46 s, 1920×1080, no voiceover, a 120 BPM track synthesized
locally and authored to the cut (the ancestor of `scripts/launch_bed.py`).

## Brief

- Promise: "Notion is where teams and agents work together: capture everything, get answers instantly, and let
  agents keep work moving 24/7."
- Audience: founders, operators and product teams on X who already know Notion.
- Arc: problem → platform feature cascade.
- Look: Notion's own brand. Warm off-white ground #F7F6F3, near-black ink #191918, Notion blue #0075DE as the only
  accent, NotionInter display, one Lyon Text italic word allowed per frame, iA Writer Mono kickers. Dark register only
  for the hook and the use-case roll.
- Copy only from notion.com. No invented numbers. No em dashes.

## Beat grid

1 beat = 0.5 s, 1 bar = 2 s. Every frame starts on a downbeat. Drops at 4.0 (brand) and 36.0 (proof). Breakdown with
riser 32–36. Final hit 42.0.

## Frames

| # | Time | Frame | Shape | What happens |

| 1 | 0–4 | Hook | kinetic beat slam | Dark. "Docs here." "Chats there." "Tasks everywhere." slam in on beats while real app-logo tiles (Slack, Gmail, GitHub, HubSpot, Figma) pop in around them; everything implodes into one white dot. |
| 2 | 4–8 | Brand | logo bloom lockup | The dot bursts into the light ground; the logo springs in huge, glides up; "Where teams and agents [Think / Build / Ship] together." builds with the site's rotating blue pill. Hand-drawn characters pop in left and right. |
| 3 | 8–14 | Product hero | floating window settle | "The AI workspace that works for you." on top; the real hero video rises in a window with a 3D tilt, lands flat at 3.0, then plays; a chip "Agents working on your board" pops above it. |
| 4 | 14–20 | Capture | layered surfaces | Split 42/58: "Bring everything into one system of record." left; the real Meetings database window right, the real recording pill floating in front with a live waveform; a row highlights, "Notes captured" chip. |

## What made it work

- The hook is pain in three beats with real tool logos crowding the frame, not a description.
- The name lands on the first drop; the second drop opens the proof.
- Every feature uses the real UI, layered for depth, and develops across the frame (type, then window, then a chip,
  then a highlight) instead of appearing at once.
- Two dark frames give the bright video rhythm.
- Transitions are mostly hard cuts on the downbeat; zoom-through at section changes; one push-slide.
- Sound effects (slams, pops, typing, clicks, chimes) were written into the music on the same grid.

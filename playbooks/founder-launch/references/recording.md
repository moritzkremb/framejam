# Recording guide (send this to the founder)

Plain, one page. The agent fills in the outline from what it knows about the product.

## Outline (talk it, don't read it)

1. One sentence that would make you stop scrolling (your background flip, a bold claim, a number).
2. Why you built it, in two sentences.
3. The problem, in the customer's words.
4. "So we built [name]." Say the name clearly.
5. Walk through 2–3 things it does while you have the product open (or say them; we'll show the screen).
6. One proof point or the offer.
7. "It's live today at [link]."

Record each part 2–3 times. Keep going after a mistake; we keep the last good take.

## Camera and sound

- Framing: chest up, eyes in the top third, a little space on one side for words (we put callouts there).
- Light: face a window or a soft light; avoid a bright window behind you.
- Background: real and calm (office, room, outdoors). No green screen needed.
- Phone is fine: rear camera, 4K 30 fps, horizontal for 16:9 (vertical only if the video is for Reels/TikTok).
- Sound matters more than picture: a clip-on mic or AirPods close to your mouth, quiet room, no music playing.

## Screen recordings

- Window around 1440×900 on a Retina screen, demo account with realistic data, notifications off.
- One action per take, 3–10 s, 1 s of stillness before and after.
- Record the 2–3 things from part 5, plus the single most impressive moment on its own.
- Optional: 5–10 s of b-roll (you working, the team, the product on a phone).

## Send

The raw files (no editing), the logo (SVG or PNG), the link, and any numbers or customer names you're happy to show.

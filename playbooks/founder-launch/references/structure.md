# Structure: what founder launch videos do

## The beats

| Beat | Time (60–90 s video) | Job | On screen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cold open | 0–5 s | Make them stay | Founder close, a callout on the key word, or the product doing something surprising |
| Why / credibility | 5–15 s | Why this person, why now | Founder; a lower third; a quick b-roll or past-company logo if relevant |
| Problem | 1–2 lines | Name the pain in the viewer's words | Callout words, or the old way on screen |
| Reveal | 1 line | The name | Logo bumper (1–1.5 s), then straight into the product |
| Demo | ~40% of runtime | Show it working | Screen recordings, pushed in, founder voice over them; brief returns to the founder |
| Proof / offer | 5–10 s | Reason to believe or to act now | Metric callout, customer quote card, price, "free for…" |
| CTA | last 5 s | One action | Founder says the link; end card with logo + link holds 2 s |

## Breakdowns (bookmarked launches that did well)

- **Energy** (@gabriel1, 72 s): "3 months ago I quit OpenAI" cold open over the founder outdoors; a list of model
  names typed on white as he lists them; kinetic words beside him ("no one is using AI to do their work"); emoji and
  line-art stickers for each point; quick meme b-roll for jokes; then 30 s of the product in screen recordings with
  captions running; founder back for "happy vibe working"; logo end card with the URL.
- **Lindy Teammate** (@Altimor, 94 s): "Today we kill the AI agent" bold open; founder in a bright office; the product
  shown as floating Slack-style message cards and a meeting-join card on a soft gradient (not raw full-screen);
  captions throughout.
- **Tapkit** (@tj_littlejohn, 46 s): founder to camera against brick; cuts to a split layout (Mac app left, iPhone
  right) showing the agent using the phone; physical phones on a stand as b-roll; logo end card.
- **shift** (@joinshift, 63 s): logo bumper first; huge orange kinetic words ("SERVICES", "COOKING.", "FIXING.") over
  b-roll; the GM to camera with a name super; "10,000+ PEOPLE" number callout next to him; split-screen b-roll cards;
  "HANDS THE BENEFITS BACK" stacked words; "join the shift." end card.
- **Airpost** (@JohnnotJon, 138 s): personal story open ("I'm 46. That's not a cool age to found a startup"),
  testimonial heads with name supers, white-on-black statement cards between sections.
- **Cursor Automations** (@cursor_ai, 95 s): two team members at a desk explaining, alternating with clean UI
  recordings of the feature; calm, no music swells.

What they share: the founder's voice carries the whole thing; the product is shown working, not described; big words
from the script become graphics; there is exactly one link at the end.

## Cold open shapes

- The credential flip: "Three months ago I quit [big company]. Today we're launching [name]."
- The kill line: "Today we kill [category]. Meet [name]."
- The number: "We gave it 700 leads. In 40 seconds it…"
- The confession: "I'm 46. That's not a cool age to found a startup."
- The product first: the most surprising 3 s of the demo, then the founder: "That's [name]."

## Line shapes for the middle

- "Here's what that looks like." (cut to the screen)
- "You just [do X]. It [does Y]." (one action, one result per line)
- "No [old pain]. No [old pain]. Just [outcome]."
- Proof: "[N] teams used it in beta. [Customer] cut [task] from [time] to [time]."
- CTA: "It's live today at [url]. Free for [who/how long]."

## Length

Cut until every line earns its place. Most founder launches that do well are 45–90 s; past 2 minutes, only story-led
videos (a personal journey) hold attention. If the recording has more good material than fits, make a 60 s main
video and offer a 20 s cut-down of the cold open + demo + CTA.

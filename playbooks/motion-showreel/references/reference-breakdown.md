# Reference breakdown: @uxmiles, "Motion designer." (15 s)

Source: https://x.com/uxmiles/status/2104618175803609305, posted by Miles (@uxmiles), a design studio founder:
"As a design studio founder, I hate to admit it but Opus 5.5 is insanely good at motion design/launch videos. This
video took 3 prompts." 1920×1080, 60 fps, 15.06 s. Read at 4 frames per second; music measured with
`beat_grid.py`: **129 BPM** (beat 0.465 s, bar 1.86 s), eighth-note hits, the track fades in over the first 2 s.

## Look
- Three colours only: warm off-white ground (#ece9e1), near-black (#141414), one orange-red accent (#ff5a1f).
  Grounds swap between them; the accent never changes.
- One heavy grotesk (tight tracking) for everything, plus a small grey label face.
- **One hero object:** an orange dot. It is in every shot and carries every transition, so the 15 s never cut; they
  morph.

## Shot by shot (seconds)

| Time | Trick | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0–1.0 | UI micro-interaction | A cursor clicks a "Reduce Motion" toggle off (the joke: motion is about to happen). Starts zoomed on the toggle, pulls out with motion blur. |
| 1.0–2.0 | Letter physics | "Reduce" breaks apart and its letters fall and spin out of frame, leaving "Motion". The i's dot is the orange accent. |
| 2.0–3.3 | Squash and stretch | The dot pops out of the "i", bounces with squash/stretch, the "i" left dotless. |
| 3.3–4.9 | Easing as data | The dot rides a spring curve on a small chart; the overshoot is labelled "+22%". |
| 4.9–5.6 | Motion trail | The curve becomes a dotted motion path; the camera follows the dot; the dot grows. |
| 5.6–6.0 | Ground flip | Hard cut on a hit: off-white → black, the dot now big in the centre. |
| 6.0–7.5 | Shape morph + colour swap | Orange ground, black circle; on each beat the circle morphs: rounded diamond → tilted rounded rectangle → pill, with an outline ghost of the previous shape. |
| 7.5–8.7 | UI again | The pill becomes an outlined pill (a toggle/progress track), shrinks back to the dot. |
| 8.7–10.5 | 3D kinetic type | A ring of words orbits the dot in perspective: "KINETIC TYPE · 3D · SOUND · LOOPS · SPRINGS · EASING", the back half mirrored and grey. |
| 10.5–12.0 | Halftone ripple | A dot grid fills the frame; a shockwave ripples out from the hero dot, the dots swell until the frame floods black. |
| 12.0–14.3 | Lockup | "Opus 5.5 / Motion designer." with the hero dot as the full stop; "0 keyframes" lands with an orange 0. |
| 14.3–15.0 | Rewind | A fast blurred rewind through the earlier shots back to the toggle: the piece loops. |

## Why it works
- **Continuity:** the hero object means no shot ever "starts"; every trick is a new thing the same object does.
- **Density:** one trick per half-bar to bar (0.9–1.9 s), no holds except the lockup.
- **Each trick names a motion-design skill** (UI, physics, easing, trails, morphs, 3D type, generative grids), so
  the piece is a capability list you watch rather than read. The ring literally spells the list.
- **Restraint:** 3 colours, 1 typeface, flat shapes, so speed reads as precision, not noise.
- **A joke at both ends:** "Reduce Motion" switched off at the start, "0 keyframes" at the end, then a loop.
- **Music:** fast (129 BPM) and percussive; cuts and morph peaks land on beats, the flips on downbeats.

## The four beats

1. **Hook, 0–5 s.** One sentence that states the news or the result, with a number or a strong verdict, while the
   proof is on screen (the launch post with its video playing, the product, the finished thing). Patterns:
   - News + number: "Yesterday X was released that's 20 to 200 times faster..."
   - Verdict: "Motion graphics is officially solved."
   - Self-proof: "The video you're seeing right now is fully edited with AI." (the intro itself is the demo)
   - Launch + pick: "OpenAI launched a bunch of things yesterday. ... But the one thing I'm most excited about is X."
2. **Context, 5–12 s.** Why it matters or what was hard before, in one or two sentences: "Up until now, video
   editing has been something AI struggled with. Well, that changed with Astra." / "It's a completely different kind
   of model, trained in a different way from an LLM."
3. **Promise, 12–28 s.** "So in this video I want to show you..." followed by exactly three things, usually *what it
   is → how to use it → examples or use cases* ("I'll show you a couple of examples, give you five business use
   cases, and explain how it works under the hood"). Each item gets its own visual.
4. **Handoff, 1–2 s.** "So let's dive in." Then a hard cut into the body, and the layout changes (framed or
   full-frame camera → full-screen recording with a small camera bubble). No title card, no logo sting, no subscribe
   ask in the intro.

Optional, evergreen tutorials only: a one-line credential before the handoff ("If you don't know me, I'm Moritz, a
German engineer with 10 years in tech who now builds AI automations for businesses"). Skip it for news videos.

Delivery: spoken straight to camera, fast, no dead air. Contractions, plain words, "guys" once at most. Intro takes
are recorded separately from the body (closer camera, louder and drier voice than the screen-recorded body).

## Layout A: edited talking head (the "fully edited" intro)

- Full-frame camera, subject at desk, centred. Framing alternates per sentence between a wide (100%) and a punch-in
  (~115–125%, face higher in frame), cut on sentence boundaries; never two consecutive shots at the same scale.
- Silences and retakes removed (pauses ≤ ~0.12 s).
- **Keyword captions**, not subtitles: 1–4 words of the sentence's key idea, bottom-left, heavy uppercase sans,
  white with the payoff word in the accent yellow, a small mono kicker above ("THIS JUST LAUNCHED") and a short
  accent underline. Bottom 30% of the frame darkened with a soft gradient. Examples: "FULLY EDITED", "ASTRA",
  "3 TOOLS. ONE TEST."
- **One full-screen insert** (3–4 s) on the context line: a designed motion graphic of the problem (an editor
  timeline UI with "VIDEO EDITING. THE HARD PART.", a yellow "+ AI" pill, a playhead, a "STILL STRUGGLING." tag),
  dark green-black ground (#0c160f, panels #1c281f), accent yellow (#efd245), mono labels.
- **Pop-up cards** for every "like this": a rounded card with the example (a frame or clip of the long-form and the
  short-form result) slides in beside the speaker, labelled in mono ("LONG-FORM 16:9", "SHORT-FORM 9:16"), timed to
  the word.
- **Icon row** for a list: app-icon tiles with names pop in one by one along the bottom when the three tools are
  named.
- Sound: a quiet music bed under the voice (gap floor ~20 dB above the body's), whoosh/pop on each graphic, the bed
  ends on "let's dive in".

## Layout B: framed screen (news, launches, reactions)

- The screen recording inset at ~86% width (x 210–1870, y 73–1007 of 1920×1080) with ~26 px rounded corners and a
  soft shadow on a blurred glowing teal/blue backdrop.
- The camera as a tall rounded card (~316×556, radius ~38) overlapping the screen's left edge, vertically centred.
- The screen shows the proof while he talks: the announcement post with its video playing, the product page, the
  feature. Hand-drawn arrows or underlines appear on the key line when it's spoken (e.g. two blue arrows at
  "20-200x faster / 40-400x cheaper").
- Static framing, no captions, no music. Hard cut to the full-screen body at "let's dive in".

## Pick

- Have a camera take of the intro and want it "edited with graphics": Layout A.
- Recording the intro over the screen, news or reaction video: Layout B.
- Both are 16:9. Target 20–35 s; up to 60 s only when the promise teases several results that each need a beat.

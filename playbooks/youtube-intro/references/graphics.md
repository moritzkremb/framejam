# Graphics and sound spec (Layout A)

Sizes are for 1920×1080. "Accent" is the style's accent colour (default yellow #efd245). Fonts default to a heavy
grotesk for captions (Inter Black / Archivo Black / the style's display face) and a mono for labels (Geist Mono,
JetBrains Mono, the style's mono). All of it is built in whatever tool the project uses; a Hyperframes example is at
the end.

## Timing rules

- Every graphic is anchored to a word in `words_cut.json`: it lands 2–4 frames **before** the word's start, so it
  reads as the word is heard.
- At most one graphic on screen besides the caption. A new graphic replaces the old one.
- Nothing static for more than ~2.5 s: alternate punch-in scale, swap the caption, or bring a card.
- Last 1 s before "let's dive in" is clean (caption only or nothing), then hard cut to the body.

## Punch-ins

- Two scales: wide 1.00 and punch 1.18 (1.25 on a 4K source), anchored a little above the face so the eyes sit on
  the upper third. Switch on sentence boundaries (a cut that tighten.py already made is the best place).
- Never animate the zoom in Layout A (cuts, not pushes). Exception: one slow push (1.00 → 1.06 over the whole
  promise beat) is fine.

## Keyword caption

- Position: left 64 px, bottom 92 px. Max width 900 px.
- Kicker: mono 22 px, tracking 0.12em, uppercase, 70% white, 10 px above the main line ("UP UNTIL NOW",
  "THIS JUST LAUNCHED", "THE TEST").
- Main: 88–104 px, weight 900, uppercase, letter-spacing -0.02em, line-height 0.95, white; the payoff word in the
  accent. Two lines max.
- Underline (optional, on names): 120×6 px accent bar, 14 px under the main line, wipes in left to right.
- Legibility: a gradient over the bottom 34% of the frame, black 0 → 55%.
- In: words rise 24 px and fade in, staggered 40 ms, 0.35 s, ease out (power3/expo). Out: 0.15 s fade. Hold at least
  0.9 s.
- Write them from the transcript: 1–4 words that carry the sentence's idea, not a subtitle. Max one per sentence,
  usually every other sentence.

## Full-screen insert (the context line)

- 3–4 s, cut in hard on the first word of the context sentence, cut out hard on the next sentence.
- A designed explainer of the problem or the before/after, not stock footage: a UI mock (an editor timeline, a
  chat, a terminal, a chart) with a big two-part headline ("VIDEO EDITING." + accent "THE HARD PART."), a small
  accent pill ("+ AI"), a mono kicker, and one moving thing (a playhead, a counter, a cursor).
- Ground #0c160f with panels #1c281f and 1 px #2d392d borders (or the style's dark ground), accent for the one
  thing that matters.
- Keep the voice running under it; the speaker is not visible.

## Pop-up card ("like this")

- A rounded card (radius 18, 8 px light border or 1 px border + shadow) with a still or a muted clip of the example,
  plus a mono label bar under it ("LONG-FORM · 16:9").
- 16:9 cards: 420×236 at left 64, vertically centred-low. 9:16 cards: 220×390 at right 64. Slide in 60 px from the
  outside edge with a slight overshoot (back.out 1.4), 0.4 s; hold until the next sentence; slide out 0.25 s.
- One card per mention, in the order they're said.

## Icon row (a list of tools, steps or results)

- Tiles 150×150, radius 28, white with the logo or icon centred and the name in 18 px below, 24 px gaps, along the
  bottom-left next to the caption ("3 TOOLS. ONE TEST.").
- Each tile pops on its name: scale 0.6 → 1 with back.out(2), 0.35 s, with a small rotation that alternates ±3° per tile.
- Use official logo files only, and only for tools that are actually used in the video.

## Sound

- Music bed: licensed or generated, calm, sits ~-26 to -30 LUFS short-term under a -14 LUFS voice (roughly 20 dB
  under), starts at 0, ends on "let's dive in" with a 0.3 s fade or a stop on a downbeat.
- SFX: a soft whoosh on each insert/card in, a pop or tick on each tile and caption, all low (-24 dB). Free sources:
  generated by the agent's tool, or a library the user has a licence for.
- Voice: the intro take, normalised to about -14 LUFS (ffmpeg `loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.5:LRA=7`).

## Layout B extras

- Arrows/underlines on the screen: hand-drawn style strokes (3–4 px, accent or #3b82f6), drawn on with a 0.3 s
  stroke reveal when the line is spoken, held to the end of the sentence.
- Optional slow push on the screen (1.00 → 1.04) during the promise beat. No captions, no music.

## Hyperframes example (caption + card)

```html
<div id="cap" class="clip" data-start="0.9" data-duration="2.2" data-track-index="3"
     style="position:absolute;left:64px;bottom:92px;max-width:900px">
  <div class="kicker">THIS JUST LAUNCHED</div>
  <div class="main"><span>FULLY</span> <span class="accent">EDITED</span></div>
</div>
<div id="card" class="clip" data-start="7.6" data-duration="2.0" data-track-index="4"
     style="position:absolute;left:64px;top:560px;width:420px">
  <img src="assets/longform.jpg" style="width:420px;height:236px;border-radius:18px;object-fit:cover" />
  <div class="label">LONG-FORM · 16:9</div>
</div>
<script>
  // inside the composition's one paused timeline; animate children, never the .clip itself
  tl.from("#cap .kicker, #cap .main span", { y: 24, opacity: 0, duration: 0.35, stagger: 0.04, ease: "expo.out" }, 0.9);
  tl.from("#card > *", { x: -60, opacity: 0, duration: 0.4, ease: "back.out(1.4)" }, 7.6);
</script>
```

Remotion: the same with `spring()`/`interpolate()` per element, `<Sequence from={frame}>` per graphic.
ffmpeg only: render the graphics as a transparent overlay (from HTML with Hyperframes, or PNG sequences) and
`overlay` them on the cut; plain `drawtext` is enough for a caption-only pass where ffmpeg was built with
libfreetype.

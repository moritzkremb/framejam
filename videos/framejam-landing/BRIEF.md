## Intent

The hero video for the Frame Jam landing page. It shows off Frame Jam's features and should be
nice and dynamic. Viewers should watch it and think "wow, I really need to try this".

## Assets

- ../../design/ — the Frame Jam design system (tokens, brand book). Brand truth for colour, type and copy voice.
- The real app (`dist/web`, served by `framejam`) — all product footage is recorded from it.

## Customizations

- Music bed plus subtle UI sound effects, no voiceover.

## Notes

- "Use the actual design footage and only things from the real app." No mocked or rebuilt UI:
  every product shot is a real recording or screenshot of the running app.
- Plays muted on the page most of the time, so on-screen type carries the story.
